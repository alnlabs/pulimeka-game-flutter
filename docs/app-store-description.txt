DESCRIPTION (paste into Description field)
Puli Meka is the classic Indian strategy game of Tigers and Goats—easy to learn, deep to master.

One side controls the tigers; the other places and moves the goats. Tigers capture by jumping over goats; goats win by trapping the tigers. Every match plays out on a traditional board with clear, beautiful visuals.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Offline — two players on one device
• vs AI — choose Tiger or Goat and pick Easy, Medium, or Hard
• Online — create or join a room with a code and play with a friend in real time

LEARN & IMPROVE
• Step-by-step visual tutorial on the real board
• Rules and hints built into the app
• Optional move hints when you need help (including after tutorial)

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• Game history and stats
• Profile with hint packs and tutorial progress
• Sound effects you can turn down or off in Settings

Whether you grew up with Puli Meka, Bagh-Chal, or Aadu Puli Aatam—or you’re discovering it for the first time—this is the board on your phone.
